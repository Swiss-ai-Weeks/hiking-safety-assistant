The grades assume snow and firn in their normal summer state, with glaciers bare enough that
crevasses are plain to see. Fresh snow or ice changes that: markings and the path disappear, firn
fields that are simple in summer become slopes where a slip runs on, and an ice axe, rope and
crampons, with the skill to use them, may be needed even on routes that normally go without.
