The grades assume good visibility. From difficult mountain hiking upwards the path is not always
visible and orientation skills are part of the grade; in cloud, markings between cairns and paint
flashes can disappear, and pathless scree or firn gives nothing to follow. Where the route is lost,
the SAC's advice is to go back to where it was last clear rather than search ahead.
