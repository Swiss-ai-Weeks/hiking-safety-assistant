The two hardest hiking grades are often or mostly pathless, with easy climbing, steep cliffs, bare
glaciers and firn slopes where a slip can mean a fall. Unlike easy alpine tours they usually have
no fixed protection, so the ground has to be mastered without a rope to fall back on. They ask for
mountaineering boots, very good judgement of terrain and wide alpine experience, including with ice
axe, rope and crampons.
