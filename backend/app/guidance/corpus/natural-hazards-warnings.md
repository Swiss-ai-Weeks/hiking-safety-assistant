Swiss federal weather warnings use one scale of danger levels, from minor through moderate,
significant and high to very high, for storms, thunderstorms, heavy rain, snowfall and other
hazards, each with advice on how to behave. A warning describes a region, not a trail: the exposed
parts of a mountain route can be worse than the level for the valley below.
