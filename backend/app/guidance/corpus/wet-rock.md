The hiking grades are given for dry terrain. Rain makes rock slabs, polished steps, roots and steep
grass slippery, and from difficult mountain hiking upwards, where hands are needed and a slip has
consequences, wet ground changes the route more than any other condition. Descents are affected
most, because they are walked tired and a slip on the way down carries further.
