Lightning usually strikes the highest point around. Summits, ridges, hills and lone trees are the
most dangerous places, as are pylons and cableway masts. Streams and steel cables conduct, so keep
off them. Open shelters and tents give no protection; a closed hut does. Put poles and other metal
several metres away and crouch low on a rucksack.
