Alpine hiking routes are sometimes pathless and need hands in places. They cross quite exposed
ground such as steep grass, rock ledges, simple firn fields and short glacier sections, and are
marked white-blue-white. They ask for experience on exposed terrain, good orientation and good
preparation: time, weather, the state of the ground and the group all have to be weighed.
