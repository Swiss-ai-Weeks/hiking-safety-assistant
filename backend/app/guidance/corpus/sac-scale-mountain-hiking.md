Hiking grade paths are well built and exposed spots are secured with cables or railings, so a fall
is largely ruled out with normal care; they are signposted yellow. Mountain hiking paths are
continuous but partly steep, exposed places are only made more secure, and a fall is no longer
ruled out. They are marked white-red-white and ask for sure footing, proper boots and basic
orientation.
