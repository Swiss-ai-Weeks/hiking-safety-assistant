Wind carries heat away from the body faster than still air, so exposed skin cools as if the air
were colder than the thermometer shows. The effect is strongest on open ridges and passes, where the
wind is highest and there is no shelter, and it grows during a long stop. Cold fingers grip rock and
cables less well.
