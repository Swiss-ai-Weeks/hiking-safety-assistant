Falls are the main cause of hiking accidents, and it takes little to slip. Tiredness and keeping up a
steady pace for a long time both wear down concentration and the steadiness of each step, which is
why the way down, walked late and tired, deserves as much care as the climb. Walking carefully also
avoids loosening stones onto people below.
