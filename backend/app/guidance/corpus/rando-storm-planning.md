In the mountains the weather can turn quickly, and a storm that catches you out is dangerous as well
as unpleasant. If you set out despite a risk of thunderstorms, start very early, know the other
routes and shortcuts, and watch how the sky develops. When bad weather is not just possible but
likely, postpone the hike or keep to a short outing.
