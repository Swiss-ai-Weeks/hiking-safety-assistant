A hiking grade describes the route in favourable conditions: good weather and visibility, dry
ground, and snow and firn in a normal summer state. It is not a description of the route on the
day. Wind, rain, cold, snow or cloud make the same path harder than its grade says, and the
difference grows with the grade, because the harder routes have fewer aids and less margin.
