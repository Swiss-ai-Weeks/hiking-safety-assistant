Summer thunderstorms grow from warm, moist, unstable air, and over the Alps they typically build
through the afternoon as the ground heats up. They bring lightning, heavy showers, hail and sudden
strong gusts, and can form within an hour from a sky that looked settled in the morning. Their exact
place is hard to forecast, which is why forecasts speak of a potential over an area.
