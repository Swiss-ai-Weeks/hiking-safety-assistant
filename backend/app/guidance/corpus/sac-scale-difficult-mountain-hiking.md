On difficult mountain hiking routes the path is not always obvious or visible. Hands may be needed
for balance, some exposed places remain where a fall has consequences, and there are pathless
stretches over scree. They are marked white-red-white and ask for good balance, sure footing,
sturdy boots, average orientation skills and basic alpine experience. Passes such as Hohtürli and
Sefinenfurgge are the SAC's own examples of this grade.
